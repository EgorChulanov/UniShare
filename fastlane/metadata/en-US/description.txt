UniShare brings your gaming world together.

Create a profile with the platforms, games, memberships and skills that matter to you. Browse player cards, discover compatible profiles and start chatting when interest is mutual.

DISCOVER YOUR NEXT GAME
Explore player profiles across PlayStation, Xbox, Nintendo, Steam, Epic Games and more. See what people play, what they are looking for and where your libraries overlap.

BUILD A PROFILE THAT FEELS YOURS
Add your avatar, platforms, games, wanted games, memberships and gaming skills. Choose a card style and keep your profile up to date as your library grows.

MATCH AND CHAT
Swipe through recommendations shaped by your games and preferences. Mutual interest opens a private conversation where you can plan sessions and discuss games.

SEARCH ACROSS THE COMMUNITY
Find people, games, platforms, subscriptions and skills from one place. Open game details and explore profiles without losing your place.

MEET NEARBY WITH AIRSHARE
Use the visible AirShare action to discover another UniShare profile nearby and decide whether you want to connect.

STORIES AND UPDATES
Read community stories with guides, feature highlights and announcements.
